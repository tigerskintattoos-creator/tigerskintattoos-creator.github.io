Devotion in ink

Shiva tattoos done with intention.

Shiva is one of the most tattooed deities in India — and one of the most visually powerful. The trishul, the damaru, the serpent Vasuki, the Ganga flowing from the matted locks, the crescent moon, the third eye — each attribute carries meaning, and each needs to be placed with care in a tattoo design.

At Tiger Skin Tattoos in Thane, we research each form before designing. Whether you want a meditative Neelkanth, a fierce Aghori Shiva, the dancing Nataraja, or Ardhanarishvara, we get the iconography right and build it into a tattoo that works for your body and placement.

We do Shiva tattoos in black and grey realism, colour, and detailed fine line styles. The approach depends on the form, the placement, and the mood you want to carry. Tell us your vision at the free consultation and we'll guide you to the right design.

Iconography we respect

Every Shiva design is researched. We get the attributes right — the correct number of arms for the form, the placement of Ganga in the jata, the third eye position, the trishul proportions. These details matter on skin for life.

Designed for your placement

A Shiva tattoo's impact depends entirely on placement. We design around the body part — chest, forearm, back, shoulder — so the figure, composition, and all attributes read clearly with the body's natural lines.

Black & grey or colour

Black and grey Shiva tattoos carry dramatic depth and timeless weight. Colour brings the divine vibrancy — the blue throat, the fire, the cosmic glow. We'll advise which approach works for your chosen form and placement.
