The studio

Behind every tattoo.

Swarain Pawar
Founder & Lead Artist · Tiger Skin Tattoos

Tiger Skin Tattoos is built on one idea: every tattoo should be as individual as the person wearing it. Swarain founded the studio in Naupada, Thane West with a clear philosophy — no flash sheets, no rushed sessions, no compromise on quality.

Trained in realism and colour work, Swarain brings the same obsession to every piece, whether it is a hyper-realistic portrait, a fine line devotional piece, or a full-colour sleeve. The studio has built its reputation on clean linework, rich shading, and tattoos that heal beautifully and age well.

Appointment-only so every client gets complete focus from concept through to the final needle. No walk-in rush, no assembly line. One client, one session, done properly. Every artist who works at Tiger Skin works to this same standard.

How it works

From idea to finished piece.

01

Free consultation

Share your idea, references, placement and size on a call, on WhatsApp or in the studio. We tell you honestly what will work on your skin.

02

Quote and deposit

You get a clear price before anything is booked. A 10–20% deposit holds your date and is adjusted in the final amount.

03

Your design

We draw the piece from scratch for your body and share it with you before the session. Changes are made until it feels right.

04

Session and aftercare

One client, one prepared station, no rush. You leave with clear aftercare steps, and we stay a message away while it heals.

Good to know

Questions, answered.

Small pieces start from ₹2,000. Custom work is ₹1,000 per square inch, so a palm-sized piece usually runs ₹8,000–₹15,000 depending on detail. Larger work is booked in sessions: ₹5,000 per hour, or ₹30,000–₹35,000 for a full-day session (sleeves, back pieces, large devotional work). Every quote is free and confirmed at your consultation.

We're appointment-only. That way every client gets a fully prepared station, dedicated time and our full attention. Message us to book a slot.

Realism is our core: portraits, devotional and deity work, pet portraits and colour realism. We also do black & grey, fine line, anime and other styles, always drawn custom for you.
