Meet the artist

Swarain Pawar.

Swarain Pawar is a Thane-based tattoo artist with 8+ years of professional experience and over 2,000 tattoos completed. He operates Tiger Skin Tattoos in Naupada, Thane West, where he works exclusively on custom pieces across realism, colour, black & grey, fine line, devotional and pet portrait work.

Swarain started tattooing at 17, and from that first session, there was only one direction. He worked his way from Thane’s early tattoo scene to being recognised as one of the city’s most sought-after artists, earning every skill through practice, not shortcuts.

His range is his credential. Swarain works across every major style, experimenting constantly, and holding each to the same standard. He holds a national convention award for colour tattooing, earned at one of India's most respected events.

At Tiger Skin, a tattoo is always a two-way collaboration. The artist cannot force a design on a client, and a client cannot get truly original work without giving the artist creative space. You bring the idea, the image, the meaning behind it. Swarain takes what is vague or unformed and turns it into something you will wear the rest of your life. That conversation is where every great piece begins.

Every piece at Tiger Skin Tattoos starts from scratch, custom-drawn for each client, never copied or templated. The portfolio is the proof.

Transparent pricing

What it costs in Thane.

Pricing depends on size, detail and placement. Every quote is honest and free. Here's how we structure it.

₹1,000 / sq inch

Most used for medium custom pieces. A palm-sized portrait typically runs ₹8,000–₹15,000.

₹5,000 / hour

For larger or more complex sessions where timing is harder to predict upfront.

₹30,000–34,000 full day

Sleeves, back pieces and large devotional work. One full session, maximum progress.

Piercing from ₹1,000

Lobe ₹1,000 · Cartilage ₹1,500 · Facial ₹1,500 · Navel ₹2,000. Jewellery included.

A small custom piece typically runs ₹2,000–₹5,000. Consultation is always free, no commitment.
