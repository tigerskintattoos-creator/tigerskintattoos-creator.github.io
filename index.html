The artist

Behind every tattoo.

Swarain Pawar
Founder & Lead Artist · Tiger Skin Tattoos

Tiger Skin Tattoos is built on one idea: every tattoo should be as individual as the person wearing it. Swarain founded the studio in Naupada, Thane West with a clear philosophy — no flash sheets, no rushed sessions, no compromise on quality.

Trained in realism and colour work, Swarain brings the same obsession to every piece, whether it is a hyper-realistic portrait, a fine line devotional piece, or a full-colour sleeve. The studio has built its reputation on clean linework, rich shading, and tattoos that heal beautifully and age well.

Appointment-only so every client gets complete focus from concept through to the final needle. No walk-in rush, no assembly line. One client, one session, done properly.
