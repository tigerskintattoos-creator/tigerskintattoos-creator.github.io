Devotion in ink

Religious tattoos done with intention.

Religious and deity tattoos are among the most meaningful work a tattoo artist does. A Ganesha on your forearm, a Mahadev on your chest, Om on your wrist — these carry devotion and personal meaning that deserves to be treated with care at every step.

At Tiger Skin Tattoos in Thane, we research each deity's iconography before designing. Ganesha's mudras, Mahadev's attributes, Durga's weapons — these details matter, and we get them right. The design is then built to work as a tattoo: placement studied, proportions balanced, style chosen to suit the subject.

We do religious work in black and grey realism, colour, and geometric mandala styles. The choice depends on the deity, the placement, and what you're going for. Tell us your vision and we'll guide you to the right approach.

A deity tattoo's impact depends on placement. We design around the shape of the body part — forearm, chest, back, shoulder — so the figure reads with the body's natural lines.
