Safe by design

Piercing done the right way.

A piercing is a small wound. How well it heals depends almost entirely on technique and hygiene. At Tiger Skin Tattoos, we use single-use, sterile needles for every piercing. Not guns, which cause blunt-force trauma to tissue.

Every piece of equipment that touches you is either single-use or autoclaved. We work in the same hygienic environment as our tattoo clients. Clean, professional, appointment-only.

We give you clear aftercare instructions before you leave, what to clean with, how often, what to avoid. Healing a piercing well is 80% aftercare, and we make sure you know exactly what to do.

Single-use needles only

What we offer

Piercing types we do.

Earlobe piercing

Single and double lobe piercings. Quick, clean, minimal discomfort. Done in minutes, heals in 6–8 weeks.

Helix piercing

Upper ear cartilage, one of the most requested piercings. Clean placement is everything here.

Tragus & anti-tragus

The small cartilage in front of the ear canal. Subtle and striking. Takes a steady hand and correct jewellery sizing.

Conch, daith & rook

Inner ear cartilage piercings that sit beautifully when placed correctly. We assess your ear anatomy before placement.

Nostril & septum

Nose piercings done with precision. Nostril placement marked and reviewed with you first. Septum positioned for clean healing and easy concealment.

Eyebrow & navel

Surface and navel piercings done with correct jewellery. We discuss anatomy and healing expectations honestly.
