Devotion in ink

Love, played on skin.

A Radha Krishna tattoo is rarely just a design. For most people it is a prayer, a family devotion, or a promise they want to carry every day. That is why we never pull one off a reference sheet.

Every piece starts with a conversation about what Krishna means to you: the playful Makhan Chor, the flute player of Vrindavan, Radha and Krishna together, or a single peacock feather that says it quietly. Then we draw the composition for your placement, so faces, hands and the flute read clearly for years.

We work in black and grey realism, colour realism and fine line, depending on the size and the feeling you want. You see the design before your session, and we refine it until it feels right.

Radha Krishna tattoo FAQs.

Small pieces like a flute or peacock feather start from ₹2,000. A detailed Radha Krishna or Krishna portrait (palm-sized to A5) usually runs ₹8,000–₹15,000. Large sleeve or back compositions are booked as full-day sessions at ₹30,000–₹35,000 each, and most need more than one. Every quote is free and confirmed at consultation.

Black and grey realism gives Krishna and Radha portraits softness and depth that ages well on Indian skin. Colour works beautifully for the peacock feather, Krishna's blue skin and the jewellery, especially in larger pieces. We'll suggest the right approach for your design and placement.

Upper arm, shoulder and forearm suit portrait-style pieces. Full sleeves and the back allow complete compositions with Radha, Krishna, the flute, cows and Vrindavan details. Small symbols like the flute or a peacock feather sit well on the wrist, collarbone or behind the ear.
