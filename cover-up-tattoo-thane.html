How cover-up tattoos work

Turn regret into something you love.

A cover-up tattoo uses a new design to conceal an existing tattoo. It's not as simple as tattooing over the old one — the existing ink is still in the skin and will push through any new design that isn't designed specifically to account for it. Poor cover-up work just adds a bigger problem on top of the old one.

Good cover-up work starts with an honest assessment. We look at the size of the old tattoo, how dark the ink is, how faded it has become, and what realistic new designs can cover it. Sometimes partial laser fading before the cover-up makes a much better result possible. We'll tell you honestly what's achievable.

Then we design specifically to cover: the new piece uses the placement and mass of the old tattoo as a foundation, incorporating darker values, bolder elements, or strategic subjects to neutralise the old design completely.
